    Ashoka Archives

    The Maze · 5 levels

    You are an Ashoka Visual Arts explorer inside the Archives after closing. Collect the Curiosity Points, stay out of the guards' flashlights, outrun the fumigation gas and reach the exit.

    Keyboard, Xbox controller and touch supported

    Developed by Lazarous Moyo · ASP26 · UG24 · Media Studies & CS · © 2026 · LinkedIn

    Field guide

    How to play

    Goal

    Reach the green EXIT door on each of the five levels. Every level hides 5 Curiosity Points — each one opens a real story from the Archives' workflow.

    Hazards

    • Guards see along their flashlight cone and never through walls. A ? means they noticed something; ! means they're chasing. Break line of sight and they'll search, then give up. Sprinting near them makes noise.
    • Fumigation gas starts at the entrance once you walk away from it, and advances one tile at a time. The HUD warns you when it's close.
    • Spike traps are instant. There is always a spike-free route.

    Controls

    Keyboard
    WASD / arrows move · Shift sprint · C checkpoint · R restart · Esc pause · L leaderboard · M sound
    Controller
    Stick / d-pad move · LB/RB or triggers sprint · X or View checkpoint · A select · B back · Menu pause
    Touch
    Drag anywhere on the maze to move · hold Sprint · tap the flag for a checkpoint

    Lives, checkpoints and ranking

    You have 3 lives per run. A checkpoint keeps the Curiosity Points you'd collected when you saved it. Ranked runs start at Level 1: A = no deaths and all 25 points, B = no deaths, C = finished. Only active play time counts — reading the archive stories never costs you time.

    Practice

    Level select

    Jump straight to any level. Practice runs are not ranked — start at Level 1 from the main menu to appear on the leaderboard.
